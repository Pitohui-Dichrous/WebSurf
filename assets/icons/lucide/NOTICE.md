# Lucide icons

Original SVGs: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/be09619c1c1124ad09c19fc3e87ee3b939409bbd/icons

31 icons are imported without redesign. `provenance.json` records their aliases, commit and SHA-256 values. `scripts/import_icons.py` mechanically converts SVG path, circle, rectangle and line geometry to Android paths and VectorDrawable XML. The common 24-unit canvas, two-unit rounded stroke and transparent fill are retained.

Lucide icons use ISC; Feather-derived icons additionally retain MIT notices. The complete upstream LICENSE is included here and in the application at `assets/licenses/Lucide-LICENSE.txt`.
