The acid-design branch uses an original WebSurf composition with the user-supplied WS.png logo. Its interface icons are imported from [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/be09619c1c1124ad09c19fc3e87ee3b939409bbd/icons), pinned to commit `be09619c1c1124ad09c19fc3e87ee3b939409bbd`, and mechanically converted from the original SVG geometry to Android vectors. Lucide uses the ISC license; Feather-derived icons retain the accompanying MIT notice. Original SVGs, per-file hashes, provenance and the complete upstream license are included in [`assets/icons/lucide`](assets/icons/lucide/), especially [`provenance.json`](assets/icons/lucide/provenance.json) and [`LICENSE`](assets/icons/lucide/LICENSE). The same license is bundled in the app at `app/src/main/assets/licenses/Lucide-LICENSE.txt`.

Resource-list fallback artwork uses the user-supplied `unknown-file.svg`, `documents-folder.svg`, `audio-eighth-note.svg`, `loading-wheel.svg` and animated loading artwork. Their embedded notices identify Phosphor Icons source geometry, Copyright (c) 2023 Phosphor Icons, under the MIT License. The embedded notice is bundled as `app/src/main/assets/licenses/Phosphor-Icons-MIT.txt`, with source details in `Resource-Art-SOURCE.txt`.

The launcher logo is the user-supplied WS.png, copied byte for byte to `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/ws_logo.png`. It is not redrawn, recolored, cropped or separated into adaptive-icon layers. The operating system may apply its own launcher mask. No QQ Browser code, graphics, fonts, native libraries or online scripts are included in the application or source delivery.
